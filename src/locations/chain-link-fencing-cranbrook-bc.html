Gate Types We Install in Cranbrook

  • Cantilever gates: Preferred for Cranbrook commercial and industrial properties where snow accumulation, uneven grade, or high traffic frequency makes a bottom-track slide gate impractical. The gate is counterbalanced and operates without a bottom track — lower maintenance and more reliable in BC Interior conditions.
  • Sliding gates: For flat-grade commercial openings. Operate on a bottom track and roller system. Standard for commercial corridors, warehouse yards, and distribution facilities in Cranbrook.
  • Swing gates: Single or double leaf for lighter-traffic industrial access points. Common on equipment storage yards and secondary access points.
  • High-security gate configurations: For cannabis facilities and government properties in Cranbrook requiring documented access control and anti-ram capability.
